Upload quality for new imports drops to medium, and story export stops being available. Nothing you already made is taken away.

8. Buying individual films

Once a month's included films are used, you can buy more as a one-off purchase. These are consumable purchases, not subscriptions: they do not renew, and they do not expire at the end of the month.

Your monthly allowance is always used first, so a bought film is only spent once the included ones are gone. If a render fails on our side you are not charged for it — the allowance or the credit comes back automatically. Deleting a film you already made does not return its credit. A film cut on your own phone uses no credit.
