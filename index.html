It finds the trips itself

You do not create trips. Rewind reads the dates and places your camera roll already records, works out which days were a trip, and names them.

What Rewind does with your photos

Rewind needs your camera roll, and that is the part the rest is built on. It reads the dates and locations already stored alongside your photos to work out where you went and when. It reads them on your phone. Nothing leaves the device until you add it to a trip — by accepting a trip Rewind found, which adds every photo it matched, or by picking photos yourself.

When you do add something to a trip, it is uploaded to private storage that only you and the people you let see that trip can read — its members, unless you open it to your friends or make it public. There is no advertising in Rewind and no analytics SDK; nothing is sold or shared with data brokers, because nothing is collected to sell.

The privacy policy sets out every piece of data that leaves your phone, where it goes, and how to get rid of it.
