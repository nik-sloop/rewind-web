The short version

Rewind scans your camera roll on your phone to work out which days were a trip. That scan reads dates and locations already stored in your photos, and its results stay on the device.

A photo or video is uploaded only when you add it to a trip. When you do, it goes to private storage that you and the people you invited to that trip can read, and nobody else.

There is no advertising in Rewind, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking across apps or websites. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

What never leaves your phone

  • Your camera roll. Rewind reads your photo library to detect trips. The photos themselves, and the metadata of photos you never add to a trip, are never uploaded and never seen by us.
  • Where Rewind thinks you live. Trip detection needs to know which photos are "home" so it can tell a trip from a Tuesday. That is worked out on the device and is not stored on our servers.
  • Stories you export. A story image is composed and captured on the device and never touches a server.

Location

Rewind's location data comes almost entirely from the coordinates already written into your photos by the camera that took them. The app also asks for your device's location so it can centre the map on you — that reading is used for the map as you look at it and is not stored on our servers.

Rewind does not track your location in the background. It has no background location capability at all; it cannot see where you are when it is closed.

Payments

Subscriptions and one-off purchases are sold by Apple, not by us. We never see your card details. Apple tells our payments provider that a purchase happened, and our provider tells our server which plan you are on — that is the whole of what we learn.

Health and fitness data

If you connect Apple Health, Rewind reads workouts and their GPS routes, and nothing else. It does not read heart rate, cadence, steps, weight, sleep, or any other health sample type. It does not write anything to Apple Health.

It reads only the dates of a trip you choose, and you select which workouts are imported. A workout you do not import is never uploaded.

A workout you do import is uploaded to our database — its type, times, distance, elevation, energy burned and route — because that is what lets the route be drawn on the trip's map and replayed alongside your photos.

We never use health or fitness data for advertising or marketing, and we never sell it or share it with data brokers. It is not disclosed to anyone except the infrastructure providers listed below that store it on our behalf, and the people you have invited to the trip it belongs to.

If you import from Strava instead, Strava may additionally supply per-second streams such as heart rate, cadence and power alongside the route, and those are stored with the activity. The paragraph above applies to them in exactly the same way.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or product-measurement SDK.
  • No third-party crash reporting.
  • No tracking you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling your personal information, and no "sharing" of it for cross-context behavioural advertising as California law defines those terms.
  • No profiling or automated decisions that have a legal effect on you.

Sharing to Instagram

If you export an edit or a story to Instagram, iOS hands Instagram the video or image file. That is a share you perform, and what happens to it afterwards is governed by Instagram's terms, not ours. We tell Instagram nothing about you.

Legal disclosure

We may disclose data if we are legally required to, or where it is necessary to protect someone's safety or to establish or defend a legal claim. If Rewind is ever sold or transferred, account data would transfer with it, and you would be told before that happened.

What other people can see

A trip is shared with the people in it. Everyone you add to a trip can see every photo, video and activity in that trip, whoever added it, along with the display name and picture of whoever added it.

You cannot currently hide an individual item from some members of a trip but not others — visibility is the whole trip. If you would rather someone did not see something, do not add it to a trip they are in.

Trips are private by default. Nothing you put in Rewind is public, or indexed by search engines, or visible to other users who are not in the trip.

Legal bases

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, we rely on these grounds under the UK GDPR and GDPR:

  • Performance of a contract — for your account, your trips, your uploads and your subscription. Without these we cannot provide the app.
  • Consent — for your photo library, your device location, Apple Health, notifications, and connecting Strava. Each is a separate permission you grant, and each can be withdrawn in iOS Settings or in the app at any time. Health data is special category data and consent is the only basis we rely on for it.
  • Legitimate interests — for keeping the service secure and preventing abuse, and for keeping records of purchases.
  • Legal obligation — where we must keep something, such as tax records of a purchase.

Withdrawing a permission does not delete what was already imported under it. You can delete that separately, or delete the whole account.

How long we keep it

  • Your trips, media and activities — until you delete them, or until you delete your account.
  • Your account — until you delete it. We do not expire accounts for inactivity.
  • A deleted account — removed from our live systems promptly and within 30 days. It may persist in encrypted backups for up to a further 30 days, after which those backups expire.
  • Purchase records — kept as long as tax and accounting law requires, typically six or seven years, even after the account is gone. These are records of a transaction, not trip content.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, export it, restrict or object to how we use it, and withdraw a consent. You also have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising any of them — Rewind works the same either way.

Much of this you can do yourself in the app: edit your profile, delete a photo or a trip, disconnect Strava, and turn any permission off in iOS Settings. For anything else, email support@example.com and we will respond within 30 days.

We do not charge for this, and we will not ask you for more identifying information than we need to be sure the request is yours.

If you are in the UK or EEA and think we have got this wrong, you can complain to your national data protection authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We would rather you told us first so we can fix it.

Deleting your account

Deleting your account removes your profile, your trips, the media you uploaded, the activities you imported, your friendships and your notification tokens. It cannot be undone, and your media cannot be recovered afterwards.

How to delete your account →

Deleting your Rewind account does not cancel an active subscription — Apple owns that relationship. Cancel it in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone, and do it before you delete the account.

Security

Your media sits in private storage — not a public bucket — and is reached through short-lived links minted for you at the moment you view it. Access is enforced by the database on every request, row by row, so being a member of one trip does not get you into another. Traffic is encrypted in transit, and storage is encrypted at rest. Sign-in tokens are held in the iOS keychain.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data and the law requires us to tell you, we will, without undue delay.

International transfers

Our providers operate in the United States and elsewhere, so your data may be processed outside your country. Where data leaves the UK or EEA, transfers are covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum, as incorporated in our agreements with each provider.

Children

Rewind is not for children. You must be at least 13 to use it, and at least 16 if you are in a country whose law sets that as the age of digital consent. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age. If you believe a child has given us their data, email us and we will delete it.
